Explainable AI for PCOS Detection and Empowerment of Underrepresented Women

My project focuses on using Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) to detect Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) through the analysis of ultrasound images, combined with demographic data, hormonal levels, and blood report parameters. This multimodal approach makes healthcare more accessible, affordable, and transparent, particularly for women in rural and underrepresented communities who often lack timely access to specialized medical support.

Addressing Healthcare Gaps in Remote Areas:
In many rural or low-resource regions, access to gynecologists and advanced diagnostic facilities is limited. Women may need to travel long distances or face cultural and financial barriers before receiving care. By developing an AI-based diagnostic system, PCOS detection can be performed locally using basic ultrasound scans and available health records, even by primary healthcare workers. The model analyzes both imaging and clinical data to flag potential PCOS cases, enabling early detection and timely medical referrals without requiring a specialist at every location.

Reducing Consultation and Diagnostic Costs:
Traditional PCOS diagnosis involves multiple doctor visits, hormonal assessments, and ultrasound interpretations, all of which contribute to significant expenses and delays. My AI model automates the initial screening process by integrating blood markers and hormonal patterns with image-based insights, thereby reducing dependence on frequent consultations. Women can thus receive a preliminary, data-driven diagnosis quickly and at a fraction of the cost, consulting doctors only when the AI flags abnormalities. This greatly reduces the financial burden and promotes preventive, low-cost healthcare delivery.

Enhancing Trust through Explainable AI:
A key innovation of this project lies in its explainable decision-making. Unlike traditional “black-box” AI models, the XAI framework highlights which ultrasound regions and clinical parameters influenced the diagnosis. This transparency builds trust among both patients and healthcare providers — allowing doctors to validate predictions and women to understand their results. In communities with limited digital literacy or medical awareness, explainability ensures that the technology remains human-centered and interpretable, not intimidating or opaque.

Empowering Women through Awareness and Accessibility:
By integrating AI explainability, multimodal health data, and cost-efficient screening, this project empowers women to take proactive control of their reproductive health. It bridges the gap between advanced medical technology and underserved populations, enabling early PCOS detection and reducing risks of infertility, diabetes, and hormonal imbalance. Ultimately, this initiative strives to bring affordable, transparent, and inclusive healthcare within the reach of every woman, regardless of geography or background.
